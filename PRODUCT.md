# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People in the Sacramento region who are curious about AI and don't want to figure it out alone: builders and developers, small-business owners, creatives and marketers, teachers and students, state and city workers, nonprofit staff, and people who are simply curious. They usually find the site from a LinkedIn post, a Luma invite or a friend, often on their phone, and decide in under a minute whether this is "for people like me."

Secondary: sponsors, venues and partners checking whether the community is credible enough to back.

## Product Purpose

sacaicollective.com is the front door for AI Collective Sacramento, the Sacramento chapter of The AI Collective. Its one job is to turn a curious visitor into a member who shows up to their first event. Success = sign-ups through the Join form and RSVPs on the Luma calendar (luma.com/sacaic).

## Brand Personality

Clear, practical, welcoming. Human-centered and grounded, never hype. The emotional goal is relief and belonging: "I'm not behind, and I'm not alone." Core idea: AI is moving too fast to figure it out alone, so people need a community, not another tool. Source of truth for voice: `../AIC/ai_collective_sacramento_brand_context.md`.

## Anti-references

- Generic "AI" imagery: robot hands, glowing brains, cold server rooms, dystopian sci-fi.
- SaaS landing-page templates: hero metrics, identical icon-card grids, fake product UI.
- Hype language: "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "disruptive", "democratizing".
- Elitist or developer-only signals that make beginners feel they don't belong.
- Faceless corporate stock photography.

## Design Principles

1. **People, not technology.** Every visual metaphor is about humans meeting. AI is the topic; the community is the product.
2. **Local, not generic.** Name Sacramento, its people and its places. It should feel like it could only belong to this city.
3. **Beginners are first-class.** Plain language, no jargon, and an obvious next step for someone who has never been to an event.
4. **Show it's real.** Real events, real photos, real partners pulled from real sources. No invented stats or fake activity.
5. **Premium craft, warm tone.** Award-level motion and polish, delivered with the warmth of a good meetup, not the coldness of a tech launch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 body contrast, visible focus states, full keyboard access (including the Join dialog). Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative. Mobile-first, since most visitors arrive from social links on a phone.
